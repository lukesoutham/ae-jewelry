The experience

Three minutes. One forever piece.

Permanent jewelry is a fine chain sized exactly to you, then closed with a tiny, quick weld instead of a clasp. No appointment drama — just sit, choose, and wear it out.

1

Measure

We wrap the chain to your wrist, ankle or neck for a fit that's yours alone — snug, comfortable, never fussy.

2

Weld

A tiny spark closes the link in seconds. You feel nothing — and there's no clasp to catch, snag or lose.

3

Forever

Shower in it, sleep in it, live in it. It stays until you choose otherwise — a quick snip and it's off.
